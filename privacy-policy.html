100% On-Device Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

PDF Toolkit — Merge, Split & Compress ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information, document handling, and device permissions.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

PDF Toolkit is built on a strict on-device privacy architecture:

  • Zero Document Uploads: All PDF conversions, merges, splits, compressions, and page extractions occur entirely locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Processing: None of your documents, photos, filenames, or rendered images are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers or cloud services.
  • Offline Functionality: The App operates completely without an internet connection for all document manipulation tools.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or user documents.

Advertising & Diagnostics (AdMob): The App uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements (banners on the home screen and capped interstitials upon completion). Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

  • Photos / Media Library: Required exclusively to select images from your gallery to convert into PDFs, and to save converted images.
  • Storage Access / Files: Required to allow you to select PDF files from your device and save output PDFs to your chosen storage location.
  • Internet: Used strictly by Google AdMob SDK to load advertisements. Document processing never utilizes the internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the Google AdMob SDK. For details, visit Google Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pdftoolkit.app.
