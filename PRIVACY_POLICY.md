# Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit — Merge, Split & Compress

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

**PDF Toolkit — Merge, Split & Compress** ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and device permissions.

---

## 1. 100% On-Device Processing

PDF Toolkit is built on a strict **on-device privacy architecture**:
- **Zero Document Uploads:** All PDF conversions, merges, splits, compressions, and page extractions occur **entirely locally on your device**.
- **No Cloud Processing:** None of your documents, photos, filenames, or rendered images are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers or cloud services.
- **Offline Functionality:** The App operates completely without an internet connection for all document manipulation tools.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We do **not** collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or user documents.

### Advertising & Diagnostics (AdMob)
The App uses **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements (banners on the home screen and capped interstitials upon completion):
- Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA if permitted), general geographic area (IP-based city/country level), and app diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
- For more details on how Google uses data from advertising partners, visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimal permissions necessary to perform its functions:

- **Photos / Media Library (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `Photo Library`):** Required exclusively to let you choose images from your gallery to convert into PDFs, and to save converted images to your photo gallery.
- **Storage Access Framework / Files (`UIDocumentPickerViewController`):** Required to allow you to select PDF files from your device and save output PDFs to your chosen storage location.
- **Internet (`INTERNET`):** Used strictly by the Google AdMob SDK to load advertisements. Document processing never utilizes the internet connection.
